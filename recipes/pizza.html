Description: Pepperoni Pizza

Pepperoni pizza is one of my favorite pizza's. The name implies the type of pizza with ingredients of it being pepperoni.

Ingredients

  • 1. Dough (of your choice)
  • 2. Robust tomatoe sauce.
  • 3. Mozzarella cheese.
  • 4. Peperroni slices.

Steps for cooking dish:

  1. Prepare your dough, toss or stretch the dought accordingly for size.
  2. Add robust tomato sauce and spread out evenly around the pie in a circular fashion typically works best.
  3. 2 cups of mozarella cheese, or more to your desired amount.
  4. Top it off with pepperoni pieces all around the pie making sure to now miss a spot.
  5. Pre-heat oven to 500F, once ready bake it for 10-15 minutes until golden brown crust or to your liking.
